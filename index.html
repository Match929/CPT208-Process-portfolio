Background and Introduction

The problem space and motivation behind Climblink

Partner Finding Difficulty

More than half of climbers face difficulty finding suitable partners, mainly due to mismatched skill levels and schedules.

Fragmented Community

Most climbers depend on familiar circles, yet interactions are scattered across temporary group chats with no lasting value.

Inconsistent Gym Info

Gym information today is fragmented and inconsistent, making it difficult to evaluate suitability — especially for beginners.

Our Solution

Climblink is a modern social platform built for climbers to meet, share, and explore together. It provides smart partner matching, comprehensive gym directories, and a persistent community layer where sessions, discussions, and relationships are continuously built and retained.

Design and Methodology

Our design process and research methodology

User Research

We conducted interviews with 20+ climbers across different skill levels to understand their pain points, goals, and behaviors in finding climbing partners and navigating climbing communities.

Design Process

Following the Double Diamond approach: Discover, Define, Develop, and Deliver. We iterated through multiple prototypes and conducted usability testing at each stage.

Prototyping and Implementation

Core features and technical implementation

Smart Matching

Multi-dimensional matching based on time, level, and gym — turning inefficient coordination into instant connection. Our AI algorithm considers skill level, availability, location, and climbing style preferences.

Community Platform

Creating a persistent social layer where sessions, discussions, and relationships are continuously built and retained. Moving beyond fragmented group chats to structured community engagement.

Gym Insights

Centralizing structured data and community insights to provide clarity, transparency, and confidence. Making better decisions before you even arrive at the gym.

Discussion and Reflection

Key insights and lessons learned from this project

Strengths

  • User-Centered Design: Successfully identified real pain points through user research and addressed them with thoughtful features.
  • Comprehensive Solution: Addressed multiple aspects of the climbing community problem - partner matching, gym information, and community building.
  • Scalable Architecture: Built on modern tech stack (Vue.js + Firebase) that can easily scale as the user base grows.

Limitations

  • Limited User Testing: More extensive user testing would be needed to validate design decisions.
  • Geographic Scope: Current focus on Suzhou gyms may limit generalizability to other markets.
  • Feature Scope: Some advanced features (gear marketplace, advanced analytics) are deferred for future iteration.

Lessons Learned

  • Iteration is Key: The Double Diamond approach helped us refine our understanding and solutions progressively.
  • Balance Ambition with Feasibility: Prioritizing core features first allowed us to deliver value quickly while planning for expansion.
  • Community Building Takes Time: Understanding how to foster genuine community engagement requires ongoing effort and feedback.

Conclusion and Future Work

Summary and roadmap for Climblink

Key Achievements

Climblink successfully demonstrates a comprehensive solution to the fragmented climbing community experience. By addressing the core pain points of partner finding, gym information accessibility, and community building, we've created a platform that has the potential to transform how climbers connect and grow together.

Impact

Our solution addresses a real market gap: more than half of climbers struggle to find suitable partners due to mismatched skill levels and schedules. Climblink's multi-dimensional matching system directly tackles this issue, potentially benefiting thousands of climbers who face similar challenges.

Future Work

  • Expanded Gym Database: Partner with more climbing gyms to provide comprehensive coverage
  • Advanced AI Matching: Implement more sophisticated matching algorithms considering climbing style and personality compatibility
  • Gear Marketplace: Launch a safe peer-to-peer gear trading platform
  • Competition Features: Add competition tracking and leaderboards for competitive climbers
  • Outdoor Expansion: Extend beyond indoor climbing to outdoor route recommendations and partner finding
